Account & Data Deletion

Delete your Hestia Care account

If you would like to delete your Hestia Care account and associated personal data, you can do so directly from within the app.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Hestia Care app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your request.

What happens after you request deletion

Once your deletion request is submitted, your Hestia Care account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted from our systems, subject to any legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory retention requirements.

If certain information must be retained for legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory reasons, it will be retained only for as long as necessary and in accordance with applicable law and our Privacy Policy.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app or need assistance with account deletion, please contact us.
